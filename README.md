# climate-finance-projects

This repository contains academic projects focused on the quantitative analysis of climate risk and its implications for financial markets and decision-making.

*Note: Project reports are written in French.*

## Projects

### 1. Energy Markets & Transition Dynamics
- Analyzed the relationship between oil prices (Brent) and renewable energy markets (S&P Global Clean Energy Index)
- Built time series models (ARMA, ARIMAX) to assess the impact of fossil fuel price dynamics on renewable asset returns
- Conducted statistical testing (ADF, KPSS) to ensure model robustness
- Interpreted results to evaluate implications for investment allocation and energy transition dynamics

### 2. Climate Risk & Financial Outcomes
- Conducted econometric analysis to assess the impact of climate risk exposure on credit access
- Built multi-variable regression models to evaluate financial outcomes
- Performed data analysis and visualization to identify structural patterns
- Interpreted results to understand how environmental risk translates into financial decisions

## Tools & Methods
- R (time series, econometrics)
- Excel & VBA
- Statistical testing (ADF, KPSS)
- Regression analysis

## Objective
These projects aim to explore how climate-related risks can be quantified and integrated into financial analysis, with a focus on investment and credit decision-making.
